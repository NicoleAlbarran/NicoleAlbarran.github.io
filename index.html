I am an undergraduate student attending UC Riverside. As a Mexican American I come from two worlds. I give my all for my parents who came from nothing and gave me and my brothers everything. My dream is to own a restaurant that embodies the love I have for my roots and I hope that through it I am able to replicate the feeling I experience every time I go back to Mexico.

I have always enjoyed animated series regardless of the targeted age group, some of my best memories come from moments in those series. In fact, when I start to feel defeated or I encounter a wall in my life that is preventing me from taking the time to study or work on smaller tasks I turn on my TV and put on an episode from a comfort series. I usually choose a series that either I have already finished watching or a series that does not require much analysis. One of favorite movies of all time is Meet The Robinsons, a 2007 Walt Disney film, when faced with challenges and road blocks I always remember its message of letting go of the past and keep moving forward. I try my best to follow the message whenever I begin to feel like I'm falling behind or an event occurs that pushes me back from achieving my dream.

My ultimate educational goal is to pursue an MBA at a highly esteemed graduate school after completing my undergraduate studies to support my career goal of creating and managing my own business at an international scale. Through my educational journey I aim to understand global markets, business strategies, and cross cultural management skills that will aid me when modeling my business with the goal of having it thrive in the global marketplace. Consequently, I hope to achieve a work-life balance that ensures financial stability and personal well-being. Upon the completion of my goals I hope to be able to provide for my parents and allow them to live experiences, without financial worry, that they were only able to dream of when they were young. It is through my success that I hope to be able to repay them for their eternal support and sacrifices they have made that have allowed me to be where I am currently During my Junior and Senior years of high school I was a member of Link Crew which was a program that dedicated itself to guiding and assisting incoming freshmen through their transition from middle school to their first year of high school. As a member I would lead lessons with a team of other members in designated freshmen classrooms touching on topics of life in high school and academics among other topics for the whole school year. I would also assist with the set up and running of events hosted specifically for the underclassmen.
